## Styles found

Ordered roughly by fit to the brief (medium–long, light flowy wave, slick-backable, oval face).

### 1. 빈티지펌 (bintiji-peom) — "vintage perm" · KR
- **Family:** medium-long layered / end-wave
- **Length band:** 중단발 — chin to collarbone. Explicit rule from stylists: front hair to the eyes, **nape grown out**, and the top section must reach the ear when pulled down; side hair must clear the ear or "the vintage feel is lost."
- **Perm technique:** heat perm (셋팅/디지털) giving a natural S-curl at the mid-lengths and ends only, root left flat-ish. Unlike 히피펌, **the design is fixed to this length** — you cannot do it short.
- **Origin:** Korea, ~2023–2026 wave; direct descendant of the 히피펌/젤리펌 trend cycle. Pushed hard by Seoul/Daegu/Gunja neighbourhood salons on Instagram Reels.
- **Longevity:** stylists showing "3개월째 유지중" (still holding at month 3).
- **Styling:** cited repeatedly as "손질도 너무 쉬워서" — easy, dry-and-go; balm/essence finish.
- **Why it fits the brief:** it is the exact intersection of *medium-long + light S-wave + low effort*, and because the root is left flat it pushes back cleanly with product.
- **Face shape:** not stated on catalog pages; the end-weight balances a longer face.

### 2. 히피펌 (hippie perm) / 세미히피펌 (semi-hippie perm) · KR
- **Family:** all-over curl, bohemian
- **Length:** medium to long; rod size is scaled to whatever length you have, so it works from 중단발 through 장발. Semi version = looser/partial.
- **Technique:** curl-focused rather than volume-focused. namu.wiki: "머리의 볼륨보다도 머리 그 자체의 컬감에 집중… 굵직하게 흘러내리는 듯한 모양" (thick, cascading, flowing shape). Soft version = 소프트 히피펌. Can be cold or heat; men's versions often wound strongly from the root ("뿌리부터 강하게").
- **Origin:** Korea; women's style ~2019–21 (Kim Sejeong cited), crossed to men ~2022–2026. namu.wiki notes it reads "마초적" (macho) on men; Robert Plant given as the archetype.
- **Longevity:** stylist FAQ answer — **2–3 months** typical; longer if you have natural wave or style it well, ~2–2.5 months if hair is fine and drops.
- **Price:** event pricing seen at ₩69,000 men / ₩99,000 women with no length surcharge (Le Marr Hair); regular menu at a Sinchon salon ₩88,000 cold / ₩112,000 heat, length surcharge up to ₩10,000–40,000.
- **Styling:** hairspray is commonly mentioned for men's hippie perm upkeep.
- **Caveat for the brief:** too much curl to slick back cleanly — the *semi/soft* version is the one to ask for.

### 3. 쉐도우펌 (shadow perm) · KR → adopted in JP as シャドウパーマ
- **Family:** all-over soft S-curl, "dandy"
- **Length:** short to medium; **explicitly "머리 길이가 충분한 길이가 되었을 때 가장 예쁘게 표현"** — best once the hair is genuinely long.
- **Technique:** stronger curl than 댄디펌 but less than 히피펌; overall S-curl wave; in the Japanese reading, applied to mid-lengths and ends with the root left natural.
- **Origin:** Korea; namu.wiki illustrates with 2PM's Lee Junho. Now a standing category on LIPPS (Japan) — e.g. 緩めシャドウパーマ, シャドウパーマラウンドマッシュ, シャドウパーマ センターパート.
- **Longevity:** ~2 months (KR consumer guide); 3–5 months per Seoul-tourism guide.
- **Face shape:** recommended for angular/square faces; less frizz-prone than the alternatives.
- **Fit:** the safest "light flowy wave that doesn't look permed", and it slicks back because the root is untouched.

### 4. 디지털펌 (digital perm) / デジタルパーマ · KR + JP
- **Family:** technique, not a silhouette
- **Length:** medium to long. One Korean guide states digital perm needs **12 cm+** because the heated rods are large, which is why cold perm remains the practical standard for most Korean men (tops usually under 10 cm). Consistent English-guide figure: 5–7 cm minimum for defined waves, 10 cm+ for full range.
- **Technique:** heated rod + chemical, per-client optimal temperature. namu.wiki: "흘러내리듯 자연스러운 컬" (curl that flows down naturally). Shows the curl **dry**, unlike cold perm which shows it damp.
- **Longevity:** JP salon blog: **3–4 months** (vs 1–2 months for ordinary men's perms). English Seoul guide: 5–7 months on virgin hair.
- **Price:** Seoul ₩150,000–300,000 (~USD 110–220). Japan: perm sits inside "デザイナーメンズカット＋パーマ ¥14,300 → ¥11,440" at LIPPS.
- **Styling:** its selling point is "少し髪を湿らしてドライヤーをあてるだけでカールが出る" — mist + blow-dry and the curl returns; big morning time-saver.
- **Risk note:** highest damage of the common options; not recommended on bleached/coloured hair without a strand test.

### 5. 韓国風デジタルパーマ / コテ巻き風パーマ (Korean-style / curling-iron-look digital perm) · JP
- **Family:** medium wave, glossy
- **Technique:** identical winding to a normal digital perm; the difference is the **finish** — "韓国風パーマは手櫛で解した後に最後に櫛を通して整えることでツヤツヤな面が整った仕上がり" (finger-comb out, then comb through so the surface is glossy and smooth).
- **Origin:** Japanese salons marketing the K-look, ~2023–2026 (stair:case Ginza, Ammy Ginza, tomoru).
- **Styling:** the salon sells it as "dry it with a little technique before bed and you wake up with Korean curls."
- **Fit:** this glossy, surface-smooth finish is precisely the "flowy but tidy, can be pushed back" register.

### 6. カルマパーマ (karuma perm) = 가르마펌 · JP catalog name for a KR style
- **Family:** center/side part with root lift
- **Length:** front hair must at least reach the eyes; top section from lower-eyelid to nose-tip length. **Layers should be kept minimal — thickness is required.**
- **Technique:** perm that stands the parting up at the root and flows the ends outward; sides kept simple.
- **Origin:** Korea ("카르마/가르마" = parting); popularised in Japan by BTS Jungkook and Nam Joo-hyuk.
- **Price (Japan):** **¥10,000–15,000 (~USD 65–100)**; **lasts 1–2 months.**
- **Styling:** towel-dry, set the part, blow-dry the roots up, then a small amount of **wet-finish oil or balm** to define the curl.
- **Face shape:** recommended for round faces, wide foreheads, and anyone wanting a cleaner jawline.
- **10 catalog variations (minimo):** カルマパーマ×刈り上げマッシュ / ×アップバング / ×シースルーバング / ×ダウンパーマ / ×サイドパート / ×ゆるウェーブ / ×ストレートマッシュ / ×スパイラルパーマ / ×ミディアムワンカール / ×短めウルフヘア.
- **Fit:** the ×ゆるウェーブ and ×ミディアムワンカール variants are the light-flowy ones; the whole family is built to be swept back.

### 7. 韓国風センターパート (Korean-style center part) · JP
- **Family:** center part, medium
- **Length:** medium; fringe long enough to fall on both sides of the face.
- **Perm pairings named in catalogs:** ツイストパーマ, シャープパーマ, シャドウパーマ, スパイラルパーマ, フェザーパーマ; also カルマパーマ and ニュアンスパーマ in style titles.
- **Styling:** dampen → towel dry → blow-dry setting the part line → **wax** (light movement) / **balm** (bundled definition + natural shine) / **oil** (gloss, mature) → separate strands → optional spray.
- **Face shape:** page states **oval = most versatile**; round faces get contour cover from the side strands; inverted-triangle gets softened.
- **Catalog size:** 8,440 styles under this one keyword on Hot Pepper.
- **Related trending keywords listed on the same page:** 刈り上げセンターパート, ローフェード, ミドルフェード, サイドパート, 無造作ヘア, シャドウマッシュ, センター分けマッシュ, ガイルヘア, リーゼント, モヒカン, パンチパーマ.

### 8. ニュアンスパーマ / 弱めパーマ (nuance / weak perm) · JP
- **Family:** technique — the lightest wave available
- **Length:** any, excels at medium.
- **Technique:** "緩く巻くパーマのこと。髪に自然な動きをつける"; loosest winding; "他のパーマに比べて、髪のダメージを最小限に抑えられる" — least damaging.
- **Longevity:** general Japanese men's perm baseline **1–2 months**.
- **Fit:** if the brief is "barely there, flowy, still slickable", this is the Japanese word to say at the counter. Appears constantly in medium catalog titles (メンズボブ/メンズミディアムパーマ/ニュアンスパーマ/ルーズパーマ — ANSWER Harajuku).

### 9. 波巻きパーマ (namimaki / wave-wound perm) · JP
- **Family:** wave
- **Technique:** several rods per strand wound in alternating directions so the hair undulates rather than ringlets. Hot Pepper's own magazine recommends **波巻きスパイラルパーマ specifically for medium-length men**.
- **Length:** medium to long.
- **Styling:** scatter with fingers for natural movement; soft-to-medium wax.
- **Fit:** the "S-wave down the length" look; the classic ウルフ×波巻き pairing (OCEAN TOKYO Sendai: 波巻きウルフパーマ).

### 10. スパイラルパーマ (spiral perm) · JP
- **Family:** technique
- **Technique:** helical winding around the rod; strong 3-D volume and lift; suited to stubbornly straight hair.
- **Length:** medium and long — it is the dominant tag in the men's **long** perm catalog (メンズロング スパイラルパーマ ツーブロック ボブ; ミディアムサーフカールセンターパートウルフスパイラルパーマ).
- **Fit:** more curl than the brief asks for unless wound loosely, but it is the standard way Japanese salons get movement into past-shoulder men's hair.

### 11. ツイストスパイラルパーマ (twist-spiral perm) · JP
- **Family:** technique hybrid
- **Technique:** twist + spiral; "一方に偏りのない印象なので、パーマを楽しみつつ好感度も重視したい方におすすめ" — balanced, keeps you likeable rather than edgy.
- **Length:** all; heavily used on マッシュウルフ and グランジマッシュ (LIPPS: グランジマッシュ×ツイストスパイラル).

### 12. ウルフパーマ / マッシュウルフ (wolf perm / mash-wolf) · JP — 울프컷 · KR
- **Family:** wolf/mullet
- **Length:** medium (HL12) through long (HL13); short top layers, long nape reaching the collar.
- **Technique:** almost always paired with 波巻き, サーフカール, スパイラル, or ニュアンス.
- **Korean framing:** "뒷머리를 길러 층이나 숱을 내 거친 질감을 표현하고 남성미가 강조된 스타일"; a natural-curl perm added makes it "샤프하면서도 부드러운" (sharp yet soft).
- **Catalog examples:** ★ウルフパーマ/メンズミディアム (Lond Printemps Ebisu); メンズマッシュウルフパーマサーフカール波巻きスパイラルパーマ (fifth men's Harajuku); メンズニュアンスウルフパーマ (LOM men's Sapporo); シースルーレイヤーウルフ (ANSWER Shibuya); スパイラルセンターパートウルフ (ii+U Ginza).
- **Fit:** good for shoulder length + movement; the layered top is what lets it be pushed back without a helmet silhouette.

### 13. 리프컷 (leaf cut) · KR
- **Family:** soft layered medium-long
- **Length:** flexible; nape grown long; "앞머리가 긴 기장감의 분위기 있는 스타일까지" — up to long-fringe versions.
- **Description:** "나뭇잎처럼 가볍고 부드러운 흐름" — leaf-light, soft flow. The gentle, gentlemanly counterpart to the wolf cut.
- **Perm:** often cut-only (₩15,000–30,000 for cut alone), or paired with a soft wave.
- **Fit:** the cut foundation most compatible with "light flowy waves + oval face".

### 14. 허쉬컷 (hush cut) · KR
- **Family:** layered shoulder-length
- **Length:** shoulder; softer and rounder than 울프컷.
- **Perm:** frequently 허쉬히피펌 (hush + hippie perm) as a combined menu item.
- **Note:** stylists warn about "절대 하면 안 되는 중단발 레이어드컷 실수" — layering mistakes at this length are the most common failure.

### 15. 레이어드컷 / 레이어드펌 (layered cut / layered perm) · KR
- **Family:** shoulder-and-past layered
- **Length:** shoulder+ ("길이감이 있기 때문에 중성적인 느낌이 더해져" — androgynous at this length).
- **Price data point:** a Seoul salon signature menu — 레이어드컷 + 셋팅펌 + clinic **₩290,000 → ₩180,000** (~USD 130) with no length surcharge.

### 16. 애즈펌 (as-perm) · KR
- **Family:** parting perm, forward-falling
- **Length:** medium; forehead and brows mostly covered.
- **Technique:** parting perm whose directionality pushes **forward** instead of to the side; a bit of root volume at the crown.
- **Origin:** created ~2018, described as Korea's "국민펌" (national perm) for 4+ years running; namu.wiki illustrates with Ji Chang-wook.
- **Face shape:** recommended for round faces and narrow foreheads; also for men with a receding/M-shaped hairline since the fringe covers it.
- **Price/longevity:** ₩50,000–120,000; 2–3 months.
- **Fit:** the *opposite* of the push-back requirement, but it's the reference point every Korean stylist compares against.

### 17. 가르마펌 (garma / parting perm) · KR
- **Family:** parting perm, side-swept, forehead exposed
- **Length:** "볼륨과 컬을 상당히 많이 넣는 펌인지라 생각보다 머리 길이가 필요" — needs real length.
- **Technique:** root volume specifically so a part can be taken without the hair collapsing flat (the "납득이 머리" failure mode). Korean hair falls from the root, so the perm exists to fight that.
- **Face shape:** best for **long faces**; needs a decent forehead — "이마가 넓거나 이마 모양이 예쁘지 않으면 어울리지 않을 수 있다."
- **Styling:** more work than 댄디펌; blow-dry along the part direction.
- **Origin/icon:** Gong Yoo.
- **Fit:** the technique layer under any "push it back" look.

### 18. 가일펌 (Guile perm) · KR
- **Family:** hard one-side sweep
- **Technique:** one side swept completely over, forehead bare, unswept fringe left flat. Shorter fringe = cool/sharp; brow-length fringe = casual.
- **Suits:** men who like pomade and want a crisp, open impression.
- **Contrast:** 가르마펌 covers the M-line and reads softer; 가일펌 exposes.
- **JP equivalent:** ガイルヘア, listed among Hot Pepper's men's trending keywords.

### 19. 올백펌 / 슬릭백 (all-back perm / slick back) · KR — かきあげ / オールバック · JP
- **Family:** pushed-back
- **Length:** works from short through 장발; the long-hair version is a named YouTube genre ("Long-haired Slickback Styling").
- **Technique:** modern 슬릭백 = short/undercut sides with only the top swept back; classic 올백머리 = everything back with the sides left long. **다운펌 on the sides is called mandatory for straight (직모) hair.**
- **Japan:** OZmall runs a dedicated men's **かきあげ前髪 × パーマ** catalog (536 styles / 20 pages); pairs with アップバング, センターパート, ニュアンスパーマ, フェザーパーマ.
- **Fit:** this is the explicit "also slicked back" half of the brief. In Japan ask for **かきあげ**, in Korea **슬릭백 / 올백펌**, in Taiwan **韓系後收64分**.

### 20. 젤리펌 (jelly perm) · KR
- **Family:** glossy defined wave
- **Length:** medium+.
- **Technique:** bouncier and more defined than a wave perm; named for the glossy, jelly-like look when wet.
- **Origin:** Seoul, breaking out 2025 → 2026; menu'd alongside 히피펌 and 구름펌 at the same price tier (₩88,000 cold / ₩112,000 heat at one Sinchon salon).
- **Longevity:** 3–5 months.

### 21. 스왈로펌 / 호일펌 (swallow / foil perm) · KR
- **Family:** volume/vintage
- **Technique:** strand twisted **from the root**, then wrapped in foil or paper. Curl looks weak but volume is enormous; vintage impression.
- **Icon:** Seo Kang-joon.
- **Relatives:** 웨트펌 (wet perm) is considered a sub-type; 그런지펌 is the messier cousin.
- **CN/TW equivalent:** 錫紙燙 / 渣男燙 uses the same twist-and-foil mechanic.

### 22. 다운펌 (down perm) / ダウンパーマ / 壓貼燙 · KR·JP·TW
- **Family:** corrective add-on, all countries
- **Technique:** reductive treatment pressing sides/nape flat. namu.wiki notes the chemical unavoidably touches the scalp on non-heat down perms.
- **Price:** KR add-on ₩20,000–40,000; one Seoul barber quoted **cut + down perm ₩65,000 → ₩50,000**, a Juno Hair customer reported **cut + down perm ₩70,000**; a Korean salon price list shows partial ₩11,000 / line ₩22,000 / full ₩33,000. Taiwan: two-sides NT$300, sides+back NT$600, full head NT$1,000.
- **Fit:** essential companion for a push-back at medium length, otherwise the sides balloon.

### 23. 볼륨펌 / 댄디펌 (volume perm / dandy perm) · KR
- **Volume perm:** weakest curl of all; definition is so loose that "볼륨펌 해주세요" gets a different result at every salon — **bring a photo**. Stylists dislike it because the curl drops fast and clients complain; they recommend perming to shadow-perm strength and letting it relax into volume-perm looseness.
- **Dandy perm:** gentle C-curl on a dandy cut. Wash and dry, no styling. Icon: Lee Dong-wook.

### 24. 시스루펌 (see-through perm) · KR
- Airy, thin fringe; "이마가 전부 드러나지 않아 앞머리의 답답함을 최소화" — suits both narrow and very wide foreheads. JP equivalent: シースルーバング / シースルーレイヤー.

### 25. ピンパーマ / アイロンパーマ / スペインカール · JP (secondary techniques)
- **ピンパーマ:** no rod — strand wrapped in paper, finger-curled, pinned at the root. Loose casual waves; beginner-friendly; pomade finish.
- **アイロンパーマ:** 160–180 °C iron + pressure. Clean defined flow lines that hold; the business-safe option; JP analogue of 아이롱펌.
- **スペインカール:** fine spiral engineered to read as natural foreign frizz; short-to-medium; gel/pomade.

### 26. マガジンパーマ + マンバン (magazine perm + man bun) · JP long
- SWELL's catalog entry "マンバンを最大限に活かす！！◇マガジンパーマ◇" is the past-shoulder end of the Japanese men's catalog — perm chosen so the hair looks good both down and tied.

### 27. 纹理烫 (texture perm) · CN
- **Family:** the Chinese default men's perm
- **Length:** short to medium-short.
- **Technique:** hair wrapped in tissue paper and curled; produces soft natural bend rather than defined curl.
- **Face shape:** "几乎适合所有的发质和脸型" — suits nearly all hair and face types; specifically good for 瓜子脸 (oval/seed-shaped).
- **Direction versatility (this is the key line for the brief):** combed **back** = 稳重大气 (composed), **forward** = 阳光减龄 (youthful), **parted** = 时尚商务 (business). Modern 中分/二八分/三七分/四六分 are all built on the texture-perm base.
- **Styling:** "不需要任何专业性的技巧" — wash, blow dry, a bit of wax.

### 28. 锡纸烫 (tin-foil perm) · CN
- Strand twisted into a rope and wrapped in foil; produces linear strand-bundles, not curls. All hair combed forward. Needs **thick** hair; on thin hair it puffs and widens the face. Suits angular faces. Requires much more daily styling time than 纹理烫. Chinese media now runs "stop getting 锡纸烫" thinkpieces — it is the dated option.

### 29. 韓系波紋捲 / 紳士波紋卷 (Korean / gentleman wave curl) · TW
- Taiwanese salon-menu name for the loose S-wave; sits alongside 韓系紋理燙, 韓系微分碎蓋, 韓系後收64分, 韓系壓貼燙 on a single men's menu (SCOII HAIR, Taichung). 手棒燙 (hand-rod hot perm) is the technique, ~2 hours in the chair.

### 30. 中分瀏海 (center-part fringe) · TW
- **Face shape:** "中分瀏海不挑臉型" — does not discriminate by face shape, but the fringe must reach or cover the brows to actually slim the face.
- **C字中分** (C-shaped center part) is called out as more natural and easier to manage than the S-shaped version, and it covers a wide forehead and prominent cheekbones.
- **Styling:** volumising spray, light wax, hair essence, or a curling wand — **avoid strong-hold gel**, it kills the natural movement.

## Gaps

1. **Naver is fully unreachable** — `search.naver.com` is blocked to WebFetch and blocked by browser safety policy; `blog.naver.com` is blocked to the Anthropic crawler. All Korean stylist blog detail reached here came via Google snippets of Naver posts, not the posts themselves. To read Korean stylist blogs directly, someone will have to open Naver manually.
2. **Kakao Hairshop (hairshop.kakao.com) was never reached** — it is the canonical Korean salon style catalog with per-menu prices. mamedene.com is its media arm and works, but its shop/menu pages are JS-rendered and returned empty to WebFetch.
3. **No Korean chain price list captured in structured form** — 준오헤어 / 차홍 / 이철헤어커커 official price pages were not retrieved. All KR prices above are individual-salon Instagram menus, a Blind post, and one English tourist guide; treat as indicative, not official.
4. **Rod sizes are almost never published.** The only numeric figures found are 0.3 cm (tight) to 3.2 cm (loose) from an English guide, and 120–180 °C for 셋팅펌. No Japanese or Korean catalog page states a rod size for a named style. This will have to come from a stylist consultation.
5. **Length in centimetres is rarely stated.** Two data points only: digital perm needs 12 cm+ (KR guide), and 5–7 cm minimum for defined waves / 6–8 cm recommended before booking (English guide). Japanese catalogs use silhouette bands (HL12 medium / HL13 long) rather than measurements.
6. **Explicit face-shape guidance for oval faces is thin.** Only mens-hairstyle.jp states it outright (oval = most versatile for center part) and StyleMap says center-part fringe "doesn't discriminate by face shape". Korean pages index on forehead width and hairline, not face shape.
7. **Chinese/Taiwanese coverage is deliberately shallow** per the brief. 2006hairsalon's 16-style list and Zhihu's perm explainers were both blocked/empty and would need a browser pass.
8. **No verified image files were collected** — only catalog page URLs, per instruction. Style thumbnails on Hot Pepper are behind the listing pages; individual style detail URLs (`/CSP/…/styleDetail/…`) were not enumerated.
9. **The strongest single lead for the brief is under-documented:** 빈티지펌 has clear length rules and a 3-month hold from stylist posts, but no salon menu with a published price for it was found — it appears bundled into 히피펌/젤리펌 pricing tiers.
